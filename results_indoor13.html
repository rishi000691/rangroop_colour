Deep Autumn complexions are deep-skinned with warm or olive undertones, exhibiting high warmth and rich depth. Bold, saturated warm darks — dark red, burgundy, forest green, burnt orange, and bronze — complement the deep warmth of the skin and create a powerful, harmonious effect. Pale, cool, or icy shades like bright yellow, light pink, and lavender lack sufficient depth and warmth, and can look incongruous against the rich skin tone.
