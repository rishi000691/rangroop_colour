Light Summer complexions have a cool undertone with fair, porcelain-like skin depth. Soft, muted cool tones — powder blue, lavender, soft pink, and celadon — complement the cool, delicate quality of the skin without adding harshness. Bright warm colors like orange-red and gold clash with the cool undertone, while jet black creates excessive contrast that overpowers the complexion's softness.
