True Autumn (Warm Autumn) skin has a distinctly warm, olive or golden undertone at medium depth. Rich, earthy warm tones — chocolate, burnt orange, goldenrod, olive green, and sienna — amplify the skin's natural warmth and create a rich, grounded look. Cool, light, or pastel shades like lavender, light sky blue, and hot pink conflict with the warm undertone and can make the complexion appear greenish or ashy.
