Light Spring complexions have a warm undertone paired with fair to light skin, giving a delicate, sunlit quality. These palettes emphasize warm yet gentle colors — soft peach, golden yellow, warm pastels, and light greens — that echo the warmth in the skin without overwhelming the lightness. Heavy darks like black, deep purple, and burgundy create too stark a contrast and can make the complexion look washed-out or sallow.
